# FD-808 Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

FD-808 is an iPad beat-making app published by BLKMKT. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: your beats, samples and recordings stay on your iPad unless you join or run a live class, and live-class data is deleted automatically after 30 days.

## Information that stays on your device

Projects, samples, microphone recordings, synth patches, settings and learning progress are stored only on your iPad. FD-808 has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the advertising identifier. Microphone audio is recorded only when you start a recording, and it is saved into your project on the device.

Crash and performance diagnostics provided by Apple (MetricKit) are kept on your device. They leave it only if you choose "Copy / share diagnostics" in Settings and send them somewhere yourself.

## Live classes

Live classes are optional. When a teacher starts a class or a student joins one, FD-808 exchanges the following with our hosting provider, Supabase (servers in the United States):

- **A class code and a random device identifier.** The identifier is generated by FD-808 and is not linked to your Apple ID or any other app.
- **The display name the student chooses.** It is shown to the teacher. Students should use a first name or nickname, not a full name.
- **Beats a student submits to the teacher.** This includes the beat title, a rendered audio file, and any feedback the teacher writes back.
- **Live session messages.** These are the musical edits the teacher broadcasts to students' iPads. They are relayed, not stored.

This information is used only to run the class. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles.

## Retention and deletion

A teacher can remove a student from a class, delete an individual submission, or delete all of a class's data at any time from the Teacher screen. Deletion is permanent and includes the stored audio. Whatever a teacher does not delete is removed automatically 30 days after the class was last active.

Because there are no accounts, we cannot look up a person's data by name. To request deletion of a class's data, ask the teacher or contact us with the class code.

## Children and schools

FD-808 is designed so that students do not need to provide personal information: no email address, account or real name is required. Schools and teachers who use live classes with students under 13 are responsible for obtaining any consent their school policies or local laws require, as they would for any classroom tool. Teachers can delete a class's data at any time, and it expires after 30 days regardless.

## Security

All network traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Class data is accessible only with the class's teacher token or a student's own token, and stored audio is available only through short-lived signed links.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at https://github.com/detroitvr221-hub/FB-808/issues. Never include a student's name in a public issue; give the class code only.
